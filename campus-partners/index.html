Submit a campus event

Who this is for

This page is for student-led clubs, fest committees, workshop hosts, college societies, event teams and campus representatives who want to make public event information easier for students to discover.

What you can share

  • College festivals, cultural programmes and campus celebrations.
  • Workshops, guest talks, seminars and skill-building sessions.
  • Hackathons, coding contests, project showcases and competitions.
  • Sports fixtures, tournaments and student activities.
  • Public club orientations, community meet-ups and campus initiatives.

How event submissions work

  1. Prepare accurate information. Include the organiser, campus, dates, timings, venue, eligibility and an official event or registration URL.
  2. Submit the event. Use the Submit an Event form and provide a working contact route for clarification.
  3. Review. Hapn may review, request changes, approve or decline the submission. Submission is not automatic publication.
  4. Keep details current. Tell the organiser and Hapn about schedule, venue, registration or cancellation changes promptly.

What makes a useful listing?

  • A specific event title and named organising club, department or team.
  • Correct start and end dates and local time zone where relevant.
  • A precise venue or clear online attendance instructions.
  • A concise description explaining who can attend and what participants will do.
  • An official registration page or organiser contact for event questions.
  • A poster that the organiser has permission to share.

Transparency and responsibilities

Hapn is an event discovery and campus community platform. It is not automatically the event host, sponsor, ticket seller, university representative or registration authority. Review is a moderation and completeness step, not independent verification of every statement in a submission. Event organisers remain responsible for event arrangements, eligibility, fees, safety, permissions and current registration details.

Submitting an event does not create an official partnership, endorsement or affiliation between Hapn and a college, university, club or organisation. Do not use another institution's name or logo in a way that suggests approval without permission.

Read the Editorial & Event Listing Standards and Terms & Conditions before submitting.

Frequently asked questions

Who can submit an event to Hapn?

Student organisers, campus clubs, event teams and college representatives can submit public college or university event details for review. Submission does not automatically establish an official partnership or endorsement.

Does submitting an event guarantee publication?

No. Submissions are reviewed for suitability and completeness. Hapn may request clarification, decline a submission or remove a listing that is inaccurate or no longer relevant.

What details should an organiser provide?

Provide the event title, organiser, participating campus, accurate date and time, venue or online format, clear description, poster where available, eligibility and an official registration or information link.

Does Hapn verify or organise submitted events?

Hapn provides event discovery and moderation. Submission review is not independent verification of every event claim, and Hapn is not automatically the organiser, ticket seller or registration authority.

How can a campus club discuss a collaboration?

A campus club or organiser can contact Hapn through the public support email and describe the campus, organisation, proposed activity and official public links. No partnership should be assumed until both parties confirm it.
