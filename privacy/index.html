Privacy Policy

Understand what information Hapn uses and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hapn (“Hapn”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit hapn.info or use its event and fest discovery, Hapn Connect, Anonymous Chat, account and related features. Please also read our Terms & Community Guidelines.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account and sign-in information: When you choose Google sign-in, Google may provide basic profile information such as your name, email address and profile image, subject to the permissions shown during sign-in. We use Supabase authentication to manage sign-in sessions. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Campus information: If you use college-restricted features, we may process the college or campus you select, eligibility or verification status, display name, academic details you choose to provide and contact details you submit.
  • Content you submit: This can include event submissions, community posts, comments, connection requests, chat messages, reports, feedback and information or files you choose to upload.
  • Technical and usage information: Our hosting, security and analytics services may process information such as IP address, browser/device details, approximate usage events, pages visited, timestamps, error logs and cookie or similar identifiers.
  • Notifications: If you enable browser or device notifications, relevant subscription or device tokens may be processed to deliver them. You can control notification permissions in your browser or device settings.

2. How we use information

We use information as reasonably necessary to operate Hapn, authenticate accounts, apply campus eligibility, display events and community content, enable chat and connection features, deliver requested notifications, respond to support requests, moderate reports, prevent spam and abuse, secure the service, understand reliability and improve the experience, and comply with applicable law.

3. Google sign-in and service providers

If you select “Continue with Google”, Google handles its own account-selection and consent process. Hapn receives only the information authorised through that process and uses it for sign-in and account-related features. Google’s handling of information is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Hapn uses third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate features. These may include Supabase for authentication and database/storage services, website hosting and delivery providers, Google Analytics for site measurement, and notification providers when notifications are enabled. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not publish passwords or secret service keys in the website interface.

4. Public posts, chat and information you share

Information you submit to public event listings, public community boards, comments or community discussions may be visible to other visitors or eligible members. Information shared in a chat or connection feature is available to the participants and may be reviewed where necessary for reports, moderation, safety, security or legal compliance. A nickname or anonymous display name is not a guarantee of complete anonymity.

Do not post passwords, one-time codes, payment credentials, identity documents, private addresses, sensitive personal information or another person’s contact details. Do not assume chat is end-to-end encrypted or use it as an emergency channel.

5. Cookies, analytics and local storage

Hapn and its providers may use cookies, browser storage and similar technologies for essential site operation, remembering preferences, security and analytics. Google Analytics may collect usage information to help us understand how the site is used. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking some storage may affect site functionality. Where a consent choice is presented, you can use it to manage optional technologies.

6. When information may be shared

We may share limited information with service providers that help operate Hapn; with other users where you intentionally publish content or communicate through a feature; when needed to investigate abuse, protect users or the service; when required by law or a valid legal process; or as part of a business or service transfer where legally permitted. We do not sell personal information for money. We do not allow advertisers to access private account credentials or private conversations through Hapn.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the relevant feature, maintain security and moderation records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations or enforce our Terms. Retention periods can differ by data type and feature; chat rooms or messages may have feature-specific expiry behaviour. Deleting a post or ending a session may not immediately remove backups, security logs or records that must be retained.

To request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your Hapn account, email collegeevent28@gmail.com from the account email where possible. Tell us what you want reviewed. We may need to verify the request before acting and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including managed authentication and access controls. No website, internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your account and device secure, sign out on shared devices and report suspected account misuse promptly.

9. Children and age

Hapn is designed for college and university communities and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Hapn, contact us so we can review the matter. Some features may have additional age or college-eligibility requirements.

10. Third-party links

Event registration links and other external links may take you to services not operated by Hapn. Their privacy practices and content are controlled by those third parties. Review their policies before submitting information or making a payment.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Hapn’s features, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version and its update date will be published on this page. Please review it periodically.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact collegeevent28@gmail.com. You can also visit our Help page.

Note: This policy is a practical description of Hapn’s current intended practices and is not legal advice. The operator should review it against the actual services, retention settings and applicable Indian privacy requirements before relying on it as a final legal document.
