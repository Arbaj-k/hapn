Editorial & Event Listing Standards

How Hapn presents public campus information, handles corrections and sets expectations for community features.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What Hapn publishes

Hapn is a campus discovery and community platform. Public listings may include college events, fests, workshops, hackathons, competitions, cultural programmes, sports activities and other campus opportunities. Community features such as Hapn Connect and Anonymous Chat have separate participation and safety expectations described in our Terms & Community Guidelines and Help & Safety Guide.

Where event information comes from

Event details are submitted or supplied by event organisers, college representatives, campus administrators or other contributors using Hapn's available publishing process. An event submission is reviewed before it is published. A review is a platform moderation and suitability step; it should not be interpreted as independent confirmation of every claim, organiser identity, venue booking, fee, prize, sponsor or registration arrangement.

Event dates, timings, venues, eligibility, fees, schedules and registration availability can change. The event organiser remains the authoritative source for current arrangements. Students should confirm important details directly with the organiser before travelling, paying or registering.

How listings are presented

  • We aim to display the event information supplied for the listing, including its title, poster, date, time, venue, organiser, description and registration link when those details are available.
  • We do not create a registration confirmation on behalf of an organiser. A link may lead to an external form, website or service.
  • Featured or pinned placement, when used by Hapn, is a display treatment and should not be read as a guarantee of event quality, endorsement or independent verification.
  • We do not publish private account-only community posts as public editorial articles or sitemap pages.

Corrections and updates

If you organise an event or notice an inaccurate, outdated, duplicated or potentially misleading listing, contact collegeevent28@gmail.com. Include the event title, its Hapn URL, the detail that needs correction and a reliable source or organiser confirmation where possible. We may ask for additional information before changing a listing. We may update, restrict or remove content when appropriate; sending a correction request does not guarantee a particular outcome or response time.

Community safety and moderation

Hapn provides reporting and moderation controls for relevant community experiences. Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information, private contact details, identity documents or another person's personal information in public posts or anonymous chat. An anonymous display name does not guarantee complete anonymity. Read the Help & Safety Guide before participating.

Independence and transparency

Hapn is a discovery platform, not automatically the organiser, venue, ticket seller or registration authority for listed events. Listing an event does not mean Hapn endorses its organiser, products, services or claims. We do not publish fabricated testimonials, invented credentials or unsupported claims of verification.

Contact

For listing corrections, editorial questions or safety concerns, email collegeevent28@gmail.com or visit the Help page.
