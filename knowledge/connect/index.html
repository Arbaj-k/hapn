Connect with your campus community

Understand Hapn Connect, a campus board for student requests, peer help, lost and found and finding teammates.

Hapn Connect (also called Share N Care) is a campus community board for student requests and updates. Depending on access and availability, posts may cover rides, lost and found, finding teammates and asking for campus help. Account-only community content is not part of public search indexing.

What can students use Connect for?

Students may post campus-related requests such as rides, lost and found, finding teammates or asking for peer help, subject to feature availability and community rules.

Is every post publicly visible?

No. Some community content may require sign-in or eligible college access and is not intended as a public searchable directory.

Does Hapn guarantee a ride, item return or response?

No. Connect helps people share requests; it does not verify every user or guarantee a transaction, ride, response or outcome.

What should I avoid sharing?

Do not post passwords, payment credentials, identity documents, private contact details or another person's personal information. Meet safely and verify arrangements independently.
