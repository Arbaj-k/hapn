Hapn product guide

Discover college events

How Hapn helps students discover published college events, workshops, hackathons and competitions.

Hapn helps students browse event information submitted by organisers and contributors and published on the platform. Listings may include dates, venues, categories, descriptions and registration links.

What events can I find?

Published listings may include college fests, workshops, hackathons, competitions, cultural programmes and sports activities. Availability varies by participating campus and submissions.

Who is responsible for event details?

The event organiser is responsible for the accuracy and currency of event information. Students should confirm schedules, eligibility, fees and registration directly with the organiser.

Does Hapn guarantee a place or ticket?

No. Hapn provides discovery and links to organiser-provided registration. A listing does not guarantee availability, admission, a ticket or successful registration.

How do I submit an event?

Organisers can use the Submit an Event page. Submission may be reviewed and does not guarantee publication.
