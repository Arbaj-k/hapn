Hapn product guide

Explore college fests

Learn how Hapn presents published college festivals, activities and organiser registration information.

Hapn's Fests experience helps students discover published college festival information. A fest listing may bring together its overview, poster, dates, venue and links to related activities when those details are provided.

What is a college fest listing?

A fest listing is a public information page for a campus festival. Details and linked activities depend on what the organiser or contributor supplies.

Are all activities included?

Not necessarily. Students should check the fest organiser's official updates for the complete schedule, changes and participation rules.

Can Hapn register me for a fest?

Hapn may link to an organiser's registration page. Registration is handled by the organiser or its chosen provider unless a page explicitly states otherwise.

How can organisers share a fest?

Organisers can submit event information through Hapn's event submission route for review.
