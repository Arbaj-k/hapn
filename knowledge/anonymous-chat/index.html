Campus conversations on Hapn

How Hapn's campus chat experiences work and important privacy and safety limits to understand.

Hapn offers campus chat experiences such as Campus Lounge, Group Chat and Ask the Community where available. Display nicknames may reduce the need to show an everyday profile name, but they do not guarantee complete anonymity.

Is Anonymous Chat completely anonymous?

No. A display nickname is not a guarantee of complete or permanent anonymity. Do not assume messages are end-to-end encrypted unless the product explicitly documents that protection.

What chat experiences are available?

Campus Lounge, Group Chat and Ask the Community may be available depending on the current product and access rules.

What information should I keep private?

Never share passwords, one-time codes, financial details, identity documents, precise location or sensitive personal information.

How should I respond to unsafe behaviour?

Stop engaging, use available reporting or blocking controls and contact the platform's support channel when needed. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
