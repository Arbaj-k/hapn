Hapn Connect — Your Campus, Helping Each Other

Find useful campus updates, ask for help and connect with students around everyday college life.

What is Hapn Connect?

Hapn Connect is a campus community board for student-to-student requests and updates. It brings common campus needs into one place so students can discover relevant posts and choose whether to respond.

What can students use it for?

  • Find a ride: Share or discover campus-related ride requests. Verify arrangements directly and take sensible safety precautions.
  • Lost and found: Post a notice about a lost or found item without exposing sensitive personal information.
  • Find teammates: Look for collaborators for projects, competitions, clubs and activities.
  • Ask for campus help: Request information or practical help from your college community.
  • Share useful updates: Post relevant opportunities and information for fellow students.

How it works

  1. Continue with an eligible college account where required.
  2. Browse the available community posts and choose a relevant category.
  3. Create a clear, genuine request or update.
  4. Send a connection request when you want to respond, and share personal contact details only when you deliberately choose to.

Privacy and safety

Do not publish phone numbers, home addresses, passwords, payment details, identity documents or another person's private information in public posts. A connection request does not verify another person's identity or guarantee a safe transaction. Be cautious with money, rides and in-person meet-ups. Use available reporting controls if a post or interaction appears harmful.

Who can access Hapn Connect?

Some community experiences require sign-in with a verified college account and may be limited to eligible college email domains. Availability depends on the campus and the features enabled for it. The community feed is not a public directory of personal posts.

How is Hapn Connect different from event listings?

Events and Fests help students discover organised campus activities. Hapn Connect focuses on student requests, practical help and community updates. You can also explore college events and college fests on Hapn.
