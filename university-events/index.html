Campus discovery on Hapn

University Events & Campus Activities

Find published college events, university festivals, workshops, competitions and activities shared by campus organisers on Hapn.

University life happens beyond lectures. Student communities organise technical workshops, hackathons, cultural programmes, sports activities, competitions and multi-day festivals. Hapn gives students one place to explore the public event information that participating campuses and organisers publish.

Looking beyond local listings? Read our international university events guide for practical ways to find official campus calendars and check event details in the USA, UK, Canada and Australia. These are discovery resources, not claims of current Hapn event coverage in those countries.

Explore different kinds of campus events

  • Workshops and learning: practical sessions, talks and skill-building activities.
  • Technology and innovation: hackathons, coding contests, project showcases and technical events.
  • Culture and creativity: performances, music, arts, exhibitions and cultural programmes.
  • Sports and competitions: tournaments, challenges and student contests.
  • College fests: campus festivals that may bring several activities together under one programme.

Find events connected to your campus

Start with the college and university events page. Choose a college from the available campus selector, then search published listings by event name, venue or category. You can also browse the published events directory for direct links to available event pages.

Hapn's coverage depends on the campuses currently active on the platform and the events they submit. It does not currently claim comprehensive coverage of every university, country or city. If a campus or event is not listed, that does not mean no event is taking place there.

Discover campus festivals

Some fests include several competitions, performances and workshops. Visit College & University Fests to explore festival information published on Hapn. Check the organiser's schedule for the latest activity timings and participation instructions.

For event organisers and student teams

Organisers can submit a college event or fest for review. Include accurate dates, timings, venue, organiser information, a useful description and the official registration link where available. Submission does not guarantee publication, and review is not independent verification of every event claim.

Student community beyond events

Campus life also includes helping one another and meeting other students. Explore Hapn Connect for campus requests and community help, and read the Editorial & Event Listing Standards to understand how public listings are handled.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of university events can students discover on Hapn?

Hapn can display published campus activities such as workshops, hackathons, competitions, cultural programmes, sports activities and college festivals when organisers submit them and they are published.

Does Hapn list every university worldwide?

No. Event availability depends on the colleges and campuses currently active on Hapn and the listings they have submitted and published. Hapn does not claim to list every university or event worldwide.

How can I find events for my campus?

Visit the Events page, choose an available college in the campus selector, and search published listings by event name, venue or category.

How do organisers submit an event?

Organisers can submit event details through Hapn's Submit an Event page. Submissions are reviewed and are not published automatically.

Are event dates and registration details guaranteed?

Event information is supplied by organisers and can change. Students should confirm current dates, eligibility, fees, venue and registration availability directly with the organiser.
