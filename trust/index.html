Trust, Transparency & Editorial Responsibility

How Hapn publishes campus information, handles corrections and explains what students should verify before taking action.

Last reviewed: 22 November 2026

Who publishes Hapn?

Hapn is the publisher and operator of hapn.info, a campus discovery and community platform. Hapn's public-facing work includes event and fest discovery, Hapn Connect and Anonymous Chat information. The platform's public support and editorial correction contact is collegeevent28@gmail.com.

We do not publish an individual editor's name, professional biography or formal editorial credentials on this page. We will not invent author identities, qualifications, legal registration details, university affiliations or endorsements. For platform and listing questions, contact Hapn using the public email above.

How event information is sourced

Event details are supplied by event organisers, college representatives, campus administrators or other contributors using Hapn's submission and publishing process. A public listing may show details such as title, dates, times, venue, organiser, description, poster and registration link when supplied.

Hapn's review is a moderation and suitability check. It is not independent verification of every claim, organiser identity, venue booking, sponsor, prize, fee, eligibility rule or registration arrangement. A listing does not mean a college or university endorses Hapn or the event.

What students should verify

  • Confirm date, time, venue, eligibility and cancellation updates with the event organiser.
  • Check that registration links belong to the organiser or an expected registration provider.
  • Confirm fees, refund terms, prizes and payment instructions through official organiser channels before paying.
  • Do not treat featured placement, a poster or a published listing as a guarantee of event quality or safety.

Corrections and complaints

  1. Email collegeevent28@gmail.com.
  2. Include the event title and direct Hapn URL.
  3. Describe the inaccurate, outdated, duplicated or concerning information.
  4. Where possible, include an official organiser source or other reliable evidence.
  5. Hapn may ask for clarification, update, restrict or remove a listing where appropriate.

We do not promise a fixed response time or a particular outcome. Please do not email passwords, identity documents, payment credentials or unrelated sensitive personal information.

How our public information is maintained

Our Editorial & Event Listing Standards explain sourcing, moderation and corrections. The Help & Safety Guide explains how to use community features more safely. The Terms & Community Guidelines describe user responsibilities and feature limitations.

Public guides are intended to explain Hapn's current features and processes. Event facts can change, so event-specific organiser information takes priority for current arrangements. We aim to correct material errors when they are brought to our attention and can be reasonably assessed.

Privacy and community safety

Some Hapn community experiences require sign-in or college eligibility. Account-only feeds, private messages and administrative interfaces are not public editorial resources. Anonymous display names do not guarantee complete anonymity. Do not share passwords, financial details, identity documents, home addresses or private information about other people in public posts or chat.

Frequently asked questions

Who publishes Hapn?

Hapn is the publisher and operator of hapn.info. Public support and editorial correction requests can be sent to collegeevent28@gmail.com. We do not claim undisclosed individual editor credentials or institutional affiliation.

How does Hapn verify event information?

Event information is supplied by organisers, college representatives, campus administrators or contributors. Hapn reviews submissions for suitability and completeness, but this is not independent verification of every event claim. Confirm important arrangements with the organiser.

How can I report an incorrect event listing?

Email the public support address with the event title, Hapn URL, incorrect detail and a reliable source or organiser confirmation where possible.

Does a Hapn listing mean a college endorses the event?

No. A listing or submission does not establish a partnership, endorsement or affiliation between Hapn and a college, university, organiser or sponsor.

Can students rely on Hapn for payments or registration confirmation?

No. Registration, ticketing, fees and event arrangements are controlled by the event organiser or the external service linked from a listing. Confirm details through official organiser channels before paying or travelling.
