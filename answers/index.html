Hapn Questions & Answers

Learn what Hapn does, how its public event listings work and what to know before using campus community features.

What is Hapn?

Hapn is a campus discovery and community platform. Students can explore published college events and fests, use Hapn Connect for campus requests and updates, and join available Anonymous Chat experiences.

What can students discover on Hapn?

Students can browse published college events, workshops, hackathons, competitions, cultural programmes, sports activities and campus fests. Availability depends on participating campuses and published listings. Browse events.

Does Hapn organise events or sell tickets?

Hapn is primarily an event discovery platform. Organisers provide event information and registration links. Unless a listing explicitly says otherwise, Hapn is not the event organiser, ticket seller or registration authority. Read event listing standards.

Are events on Hapn independently verified?

Event submissions may be reviewed for suitability and moderation, but publication review is not independent confirmation of every event claim. Confirm current dates, venues, eligibility, fees and registration with the organiser.

What is Hapn Connect?

Hapn Connect, also called Share N Care, is a campus community board for student requests and updates such as rides, lost and found, finding teammates and asking for campus help. Some features require sign-in and eligible college access. Explore Hapn Connect.

Is Hapn Anonymous Chat completely anonymous?

No. Anonymous Chat may use a display nickname instead of an everyday profile name, but this does not guarantee complete or permanent anonymity or end-to-end encryption. Do not share sensitive personal, financial, location or account information. Read about Anonymous Chat.

Does Hapn cover every college worldwide?

No. Public event availability depends on participating campuses and the events their organisers or contributors submit and publish. Hapn does not claim comprehensive coverage of every college, city or country.

How can an organiser submit an event?

Organisers can use Submit an Event to send event details for review. Submission does not guarantee publication. Organisers remain responsible for accurate and up-to-date event information.

How should students use Hapn safely?

Confirm event arrangements with organisers. In community features, avoid sharing passwords, financial details, identity documents, private contact information or another person's personal data. Be cautious with payments, rides and in-person meet-ups, and use available reporting controls. Read the Trust & Transparency guide and Community Guidelines.
