# Hapn competitor SEO and AI citation gap analysis

**Research date:** 22 November 2026  
**Market focus:** Jaipur first; India-wide college event discovery second.  
**Purpose:** Internal strategy research, not a claim of measured Google rankings or AI citations.

## Executive summary

The public search sample shows that event discovery competitors already have strong, focused inventory pages. Unstop covers college fests, competitions, hackathons and workshops at national scale. Techsnap has a highly specific, frequently refreshed student hackathon directory with deadlines, eligibility, city and format filters. FESTOOO positions itself as a broad college-fest aggregator and makes organiser submission prominent. Eventopia presents a campus-first event and fest discovery proposition with campus/event scale claims on its own homepage.

Jaipur also has institution-owned sources such as St. Xavier's College Jaipur's registration portal and individual fest websites. These are primary sources for their own events and can outrank or be more authoritative than a general aggregator for institution-specific searches.

Hapn should not imitate unsupported scale claims or try to beat large inventories with thin pages. Its defensible position is the combination of:
1. Local campus discovery, starting with verified and current Jaipur listings.
2. Clear event pages that point students to the organiser for final details and registration.
3. A wider campus-community layer: Hapn Connect and Anonymous Chat, with careful privacy explanations.
4. Organiser submissions and correction pathways, without implying independent verification where it has not happened.

## Search intent gap map

### High-priority local intent
- college events in Jaipur
- college fests in Jaipur
- university events in Jaipur
- Jaipur college hackathons
- workshops and competitions for college students in Jaipur

**Hapn action:** Keep the Jaipur landing page tied to actual published inventory. Show event date, college/organiser, category, venue, source/registration link and last-updated time when those fields are known. When there are no current listings, explain how to submit an event instead of generating empty keyword pages.

### National student intent
- college fests in India
- hackathons for college students in India
- student workshops and competitions
- submit a college event

**Hapn action:** Expand only when there is real, maintained inventory from additional cities/campuses. Avoid claiming pan-India coverage prematurely.

### Product/category differentiation intent
- campus community for college students
- college lost and found / find teammates
- anonymous campus chat safety
- event discovery vs event registration platforms

**Hapn action:** Explain the distinct value of Hapn Connect and Anonymous Chat, including access limitations and privacy/safety boundaries. These are differentiators, not reasons to expose private content to search crawlers.

## AEO / LLMO citation opportunities

The competitor pages reviewed are rich in structured, answerable information: event categories, deadlines, eligibility, locations, organiser routes and frequently asked questions. Hapn's public pages should answer comparable user questions only when the underlying data is available.

Recommended answer patterns:
- **What is happening at colleges in Jaipur?** Show only currently published listings and state the date the directory was updated.
- **How do I register?** Explain that the event organiser controls registration; link to the organiser's official form.
- **Is this event verified?** State the actual moderation/review process. Do not equate publication review with independent verification.
- **How do I submit my event?** Link to the submission flow and explain that submission is not a guarantee of publication.
- **Is Anonymous Chat fully anonymous?** Clearly say no; a nickname does not guarantee complete anonymity or encryption.
- **Does Hapn cover every college?** Clearly state that coverage depends on participating campuses and published submissions.

## Competitor comparison guardrails

- Compare public, currently observable features only.
- Date every comparison and link to each competitor's official page.
- Do not claim Hapn is better, larger, safer, more verified or more comprehensive without measurable evidence.
- Do not copy competitor text, branding, images or event data without permission/appropriate sourcing.
- A search result appearing in this research sample is not proof of a ranking position, traffic share, AI citation frequency or market leadership.
- AI citation share must be measured using the repeatable prompt protocol in `docs/aeo-llmo-measurement.md`, with dated observations and evidence.
