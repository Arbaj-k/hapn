One place for your campus life

Hapn helps students discover what is happening around their college and take part in their campus community. Browse public event and fest listings, explore student-to-student help through Hapn Connect, or join available campus conversations in Anonymous Chat.

Discover college and university events

Find workshops, hackathons, competitions, cultural programmes, sports activities and university festivals. Explore college and university event discovery on Hapn. Event pages bring together the information published by organisers, such as dates, timings, venues, posters and registration links. Details can change, so confirm important information with the organiser before travelling or registering. Browse college events or explore college fests.

Get help from your campus community

Hapn Connect (Share N Care) is for practical student requests such as finding a ride, posting lost-and-found notices, looking for teammates and asking for campus help. Some community features require an eligible college account. Learn about Hapn Connect.

Join campus conversations

Hapn Anonymous Chat offers available spaces for one-to-one conversations, group discussions and community questions. An anonymous display nickname does not guarantee complete anonymity; avoid sharing passwords, private contact details or sensitive information. Read about Anonymous Chat.

Organising a college event?

Organisers can submit an event or fest to Hapn for review. Publication is not automatic, and organisers remain responsible for the accuracy of event details and registration arrangements.
