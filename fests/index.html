What is a college fest?

A college fest is a campus festival organised by a college or student community. It may include cultural performances, technical competitions, workshops, sports, exhibitions and multiple activities under one fest.

What can I find on Hapn Fests?

Explore fest listings and the information organisers share, such as posters, schedules, activities and related event details. Availability depends on what has been published for your selected college.

How do I find fests from my college?

Use the college selector near the top of this page to choose your campus. Hapn can then show fest information associated with that college when available.

How do I participate?

Open a fest or its related event to review the published details. Follow the organiser-provided registration instructions and confirm dates, eligibility, fees and venue with the organiser before making plans.

Organisers can submit a college event or fest for review. Submissions are reviewed and are not published automatically.
