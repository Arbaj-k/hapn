Clear, respectful and safer participation across Hapn.

1. What Hapn provides

Hapn helps students discover campus events and fests and participate in campus community experiences. Features may include event information and external registration links, community posts and connection requests, notifications, anonymous chat rooms and question-and-answer discussions. Features may change, be limited by college eligibility or be temporarily unavailable.

2. Eligibility and campus access

Some community features, including Hapn Connect, may require sign-in with a verified Google account associated with an eligible college email domain. You must provide accurate account information, use an account you are authorised to access and keep your sign-in credentials secure. Do not impersonate another student, college or organisation.

3. Events, fests and registration

Event and fest details may be supplied by organisers, colleges, administrators or users. Dates, venues, schedules, eligibility, fees and registration availability can change. Check important details with the organiser before travelling, paying or making plans. Unless expressly stated otherwise, Hapn is a discovery and information platform, not the event organiser, venue, ticket seller or registration authority.

Registration links may open third-party websites, forms or payment services. Their content, security, privacy practices, fees, confirmations, cancellations and refunds are controlled by those providers or the event organiser, not Hapn. Clicking a link or submitting a form does not guarantee selection or a confirmed place.

4. Hapn Connect — posts and connection requests

Hapn Connect is a campus community board for relevant student-to-student requests and updates, such as rides, lost and found, finding teammates, campus help and useful opportunities. When using it:

  • Post genuine, relevant and accurate information. Choose the closest available category and include only details needed to explain your request.
  • Do not publish phone numbers, private email addresses, home addresses, identity documents, payment credentials or another person’s personal information in a public post.
  • Connection requests are an invitation to communicate, not a guarantee that the other person will reply, meet, provide a service or complete an exchange.
  • Only share contact information when you have considered the risks and deliberately choose to share it. Do not pressure anyone to disclose their number or move a conversation to another service.
  • Do not use posts or connection requests for spam, scams, harassment, stalking, discrimination, commercial solicitation or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Keep arrangements, payments and meet-ups cautious. Verify who you are dealing with and choose a safe public place if meeting in person.

5. Hapn Anonymous Chat

Anonymous Chat may offer a private one-to-one matching room (Campus Lounge), a shared group room and an Ask the Community discussion space. Room availability, matching and message-retention periods may vary. Follow the instructions and controls shown in the room.

  • Display anonymity: A nickname or anonymous display does not make you unidentifiable in every circumstance. Do not assume that chat is end-to-end encrypted or that no data can be associated with a session.
  • Personal safety: Never share passwords, one-time codes, financial or payment details, private contact information, precise location, intimate images or sensitive personal information with strangers.
  • Respect and consent: No threats, bullying, hate speech, sexual harassment, coercion, impersonation, doxxing, blackmail or unwanted repeated contact.
  • Reports and moderation: Use available report and leave/end-chat controls when needed. Hapn may review reported content and relevant information to investigate abuse, protect users, enforce these Terms or comply with law.
  • No guarantee of conduct: Other participants are independent users. Hapn cannot guarantee their identity, intentions, accuracy or behaviour. Use caution and end a conversation if it feels unsafe.

6. Community standards and prohibited conduct

Across all Hapn features, you must not:

  • Post or send unlawful, fraudulent, threatening, hateful, harassing, sexually exploitative or deliberately misleading content.

  • Upload malware, attempt unauthorised access, interfere with service operation or evade safety controls.
