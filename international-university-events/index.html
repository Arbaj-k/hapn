Discover campus life around the world

University events help students meet people, explore interests and learn outside the classroom. Depending on the institution and organiser, public activities can include orientation programmes, student society events, academic talks, workshops, hackathons, cultural festivals, sports competitions and career events.

Hapn is building a public discovery experience for college and university activities. The Events page and published events directory show listings that are currently available on the platform. Coverage depends on organisers and campuses that submit events and have them published.

Where students may discover university activities

United States

For a university event in the United States, start with the host institution's official events calendar, student affairs office or student organisation page. Many campus activities have eligibility rules for enrolled students, guests or affiliated groups. Check access and registration conditions before making plans.

United Kingdom

In the United Kingdom, check the university events calendar, students' union and the official page of the organising society. Confirm whether an event is open to visitors, whether membership is required and whether registration closes before the event date.

Canada

For Canadian campus activities, consult the institution's official calendar, student association or faculty and club announcements. Review campus location, seasonal or weather-related updates, accessibility information and any restrictions for external attendees.

Australia

For Australian university events, use the university events calendar and official student association or club channels. Check local time zones, campus location, ticket conditions and any changes to outdoor event arrangements.

Coverage note: These sections are guidance on how to find reliable institutional information. They are not claims that Hapn currently has event inventory, partnerships or verified campus listings in these countries. See the Trust & Transparency page for our sourcing and review limits.

How to evaluate an event before registering

  • Find the event on the official university, student union or organiser channel.
  • Check the organiser's identity, event date, local time zone and venue.
  • Read attendee eligibility, age or membership requirements and accessibility information.
  • Review fees, refunds, cancellation rules and the domain used for registration or payment.
  • Contact the organiser through an official channel if important information is unclear.

For international campus organisers

Student clubs, university societies and event teams can submit a public event to Hapn for review. Include the institution and organising group, accurate local date and time, venue or online access details, attendee eligibility, a clear description and an official event or registration link.

Submissions are reviewed and are not published automatically. A Hapn listing does not establish an institutional partnership, endorsement or independent verification of every event detail. Read the Campus Partners guide before submitting.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find university events from every country on Hapn?

Not currently. Hapn's event coverage depends on participating organisers and published submissions. We do not claim comprehensive listings for the USA, UK, Canada, Australia or every country.

What kinds of international student events should I look for?

Students may look for public university festivals, orientation activities, workshops, hackathons, research talks, cultural programmes, sports events and student society activities. Check the host institution or organiser for current details.

How do I check whether an international university event is genuine?

Confirm the event on the university or organiser's official website or verified channel. Check organiser identity, venue, date, eligibility, fees, cancellation terms and registration domain before sharing personal information or making a payment.

Can an international student organisation submit an event to Hapn?

Organisers can submit public event information for review. Submission does not guarantee publication or imply a university partnership.

Which Hapn page shows events currently published?

The Events page and published events directory show listings currently available on Hapn.
