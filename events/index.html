College and university event discovery — common questions

What types of college events can I find on Hapn?

Hapn lists public college activities such as workshops, hackathons, competitions, cultural programmes, sports events and other campus events when organisers have submitted them and they have been published.

How do I find events for my college?

Use the college selector near the top of this page to choose your campus. You can then search by event name, venue or category and browse the available listings.

How do I register for a college event?

Open an event page and review its date, time, venue, organiser and registration details. If a registration link is available, use it to continue with the organiser's form or website. Check fees, eligibility and availability directly with the organiser.

Are Hapn event details guaranteed to be current?

Event information is supplied by organisers and may change. Hapn is a discovery platform, not automatically the event organiser or registration authority. Confirm important details with the organiser before making plans.

Can I submit my college event to Hapn?

Yes. Organisers can use the Submit an Event page to send event information for review. Submission does not guarantee publication; provide accurate dates, venue, organiser, poster and registration details.

Can I find university events in every country?

Hapn's event availability depends on the colleges and campuses currently active on the platform. Choose a college from the selector to see listings associated with that campus. We do not claim to list every university or event worldwide.
