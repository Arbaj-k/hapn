What is Hapn?

Hapn is a campus discovery and community platform built to bring students closer to the things and people around them. From workshops and fests to finding a teammate, asking for help or starting a conversation, Hapn gives campus life a place to come together.

Explore Hapn

Discover Events & Fests

Find workshops, hackathons, competitions, cultural programmes, sports events and other campus activities. Explore event and fest pages, check dates, timings, venues, schedules and organiser-provided registration details. Registration and event arrangements are handled by the respective organiser.

Hapn Connect — Share N Care

Hapn Connect is a campus community board where students can share useful requests and updates with their college community. It is designed for everyday campus needs such as finding a ride, posting lost-and-found notices, looking for teammates, asking for help, sharing useful information or discovering student opportunities.

  • Post: Share a clear request or update with the relevant category and details.
  • Discover: Browse and search posts that may be useful to you.
  • Connect: Send a connection request when you want to respond to a post.
  • Choose what to share: Contact details are not something to put in a public post. Share personal contact information only when you understand the request and choose to do so.
  • Stay updated: Use available notifications to follow relevant activity and responses.

Hapn Connect is intended for genuine campus help and student-to-student coordination — not spam, unsolicited marketing or public exposure of someone’s private details.

Hapn Anonymous Chat

Anonymous Chat gives students a way to talk, ask questions and interact without displaying their everyday profile name in the chat experience. Depending on the room available, you may be able to:

  • Campus Lounge: Get matched for a private one-to-one conversation with another available student.
  • Group Chat: Join a shared campus conversation. Messages in this room may be time-limited.
  • Ask the Community: Post a question, request advice or share a campus update and take part in replies.

Anonymous Chat is a way to participate with a display nickname; it is not a promise of complete or permanent anonymity. Do not share passwords, phone numbers, financial details, private photos, addresses or other sensitive information. Use the report and leave-chat controls when needed.

How to get started

  1. ExploreBrowse events and fests, or open Hapn Connect and Anonymous Chat.
  2. Choose your campusSome community experiences require a verified college account and an eligible college email domain.
  3. Take part thoughtfullyCheck details, follow community guidelines and respect other students.
  4. Stay safeShare only what is necessary and report content that appears harmful or inappropriate.

For organisers and college communities

Organisers can submit event information for review using Submit an Event. College communities can use Hapn’s available event and community tools to keep students informed. Publication and access may depend on review, college eligibility and the features enabled for that campus.

Our aim

We want students to discover more opportunities, find the help they need and feel more connected to campus life. Hapn brings information and community closer — so it’s easier to know what’s happening and be part of it.
