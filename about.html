What is Hapn?

Hapn is a college event discovery platform built to make campus life easier to explore. From workshops and hackathons to cultural fests, competitions, sports and community activities, Hapn brings event information together so students can spend less time searching and more time taking part.

Whether you are looking to learn something new, meet people, showcase your talent or find something exciting to do, Hapn helps you see what is happening and where to start.

What you can do on Hapn

  • Discover events: Explore upcoming activities and browse by category, date or college.
  • Explore college fests: Find fest details, schedules, activity posters, venues and registration information in one place.
  • Get the details: Check event dates, timings, locations, descriptions and organizer-provided links.
  • Join in: Follow the registration link provided by the organizer. Some links may open an external website or form.
  • Stay connected: Use Hapn's community features, where available, to share useful updates around campus activities.

How it works

  1. Choose your collegeUse the college selector to see listings relevant to your campus.
  2. Find something interestingBrowse events and fests, or search for a topic, venue or activity.
  3. Review the detailsCheck the date, time, venue, eligibility and instructions shared by the organizer.
  4. Register with the organizerOpen the registration link and complete the process on the destination platform. The organizer manages registrations and participation.

For colleges and event organizers

Have an event students should know about? Share its details with your college event administrator or use the Submit an Event page. Include the event or fest name, date and time, venue, a clear poster, a short description and the official registration link. Submissions may be reviewed before they appear publicly.

Our aim

We want campus opportunities to be easier to find and easier to join. Hapn is here to help students stay informed, discover new interests and make more of their college experience.
