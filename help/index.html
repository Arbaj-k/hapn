Help

Quick answers to common questions.

Looking for a complete product explanation? Visit the Hapn Questions & Answers guide.

How do I find events?

Open Events to browse listings. Use the search field or category filters to narrow the results.

How do I register?

Choose an event and select Register Now while registration is open. The registration form opens in a new tab.

Why can't I register?

Registration may be closed because the deadline has passed or the event has ended. Contact the organizer if you need clarification.

How can an event be listed?

Ask the college event administrators to publish it. You can contact collegeevent28@gmail.com.

What is Hapn?

Hapn is a campus discovery and community platform. Students can explore college events and fests, use Hapn Connect for campus requests and updates, and join available Anonymous Chat experiences.

How do I find events from my college?

Choose your college using the college selector on the Home, Events or Fests page. Browse public listings and open an event to view its published date, time, venue, organiser and registration information.

How do I find college fests?

Open College Fests and select your college. Fest information appears when organisers or campus administrators have published it.

What is Hapn Connect?

Hapn Connect (Share N Care) is a campus community board for relevant student requests such as rides, lost and found, finding teammates and asking for campus help. Some community features require an eligible college account.

Is Hapn Anonymous Chat completely anonymous?

No. Chat can use an anonymous display nickname, but this does not guarantee complete or permanent anonymity, end-to-end encryption or that a participant cannot identify you. Avoid sharing private contact, financial, location or account information.

How do I stay safer in chat or Connect?

Share only what is needed, do not publish another person's private details, be cautious with requests for money or meet-ups, and use available report and leave controls if something feels unsafe. Read the Terms and Community Guidelines.

How can I submit a college event?

Use Submit an Event to send the event details for review. Submission does not mean automatic publication. Check that the poster, date, venue, organiser and registration information are accurate.

Who is responsible for event registration?

Event information and registration links are supplied by the organiser. Hapn is a discovery platform and does not automatically organise events, sell tickets or confirm registration places. Verify important details with the organiser.

How do I report a problem or ask for help?

Use the reporting controls available in the relevant feature, visit the Help page, or email collegeevent28@gmail.com.
